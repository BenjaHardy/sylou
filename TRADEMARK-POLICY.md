# Sylou Trademark Policy

**Version 1.261022 — [22/10/2026]**

## What this policy is about

Sylou is an open-source software, released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. The FLOW methodology that underpins Sylou is published under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license. Anyone can use, modify, run, and redistribute Sylou's code and methodology under those licenses.

There is a trademark policy for the **Sylou name and logo, and the FLOW Pledge Badges format**, but it's not meant to put people off. 

If you're reading this and wondering whether your planned use is OK, the short answer is: it almost certainly is. Most contribution scenarios are entirely unaffected by the trademark policy: PRs, branches, forks, distribution packages, self-hosted deployments, and educational use are all fine. 

The bias of the trademark policy is toward permission. In a nutshell: if you run a substantively modified or commercial competing version, we ask that you do not use the Sylou name, logo, and FLOW Pledges Badges format. 
As such, this applies for:

- Forks operated as long-term independent projects competing for the Sylou identity, or positioning themselves as alternatives to the official Sylou project;
- Substantive modifications to the methodology (such as the FLOW prompts, scoring rubric, pillar definitions) that materially impact the analytical output produced for the users;
- Modifications to core behaviors that negatively impact the characteristics of what Sylou *is*: for example, adding telemetry that violates the project's privacy commitments, or removing functionalities that support the the transparency of the FLOW methodology;
- Any use of the Sylou name or logo aimed to mislead users or in connection with content that violates law, harasses individuals, promotes hatred, or is otherwise harmful.  

## How to ask

Any doubt, email trademark@sylou.eu. This policy aims to be practical and pragmatic. If there are conditions worth discussing, or points worth clarifying, just reach out.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on GitHub. Changes apply prospectively; permissions previously granted in writing remain in effect under their original terms.
