**Version 1.260910 — [10/09/2026]**

## Why this policy exists

Sylou is an open-source software, released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. The FLOW methodology that underpins Sylou is published under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license. Anyone can use, modify, run, and redistribute Sylou's code and methodology under those licenses.

The **Sylou name and logo, and the FLOW Pledge Badges format**, are governed by this policy, not by the AGPL or CC BY-SA licenses.

The purpose of this trademark is to help ensure the consistency of what Sylou stands for: the methodology, the transparency commitments, the privacy posture, etc.
The policy is not designed to extract revenue from community use: if the official Sylou project ever offers commercial services or licensing arrangements in the future, those will be additional offerings alongside the open-source project, not replacements for it.

If you're reading this and wondering whether your planned use is OK, the short answer is: it almost certainly is. Most contribution scenarios are entirely unaffected by the trademark policy: PRs, branches, forks, distribution packages, self-hosted deployments, and educational use are all fine, and the bias of the trademark policy is toward permission.

Throughout this document, "the official Sylou project" refers to the upstream project maintained by the trademark holder (the version available at sylou.eu and the official source repository, as distinguished from forks and third-party deployments).

## In a nutshell

The Sylou name, logo, and FLOW Pledges Badges format, are not authorized on substantively modified or competing versions, or for cases that could mislead users about Sylou and what they're using.
As such, this applies for:

- Forks operated as long-term independent projects competing for the Sylou identity, or positioning themselves as alternatives to the official Sylou project;
- Substantive modifications to the methodology (such as the FLOW prompts, scoring rubric, pillar definitions) that materially impact the analytical output produced for the users;
- Modifications to core behaviors that negatively impact the characteristics of what Sylou *is*: for example, adding telemetry that violates the project's privacy commitments, or removing functionalities that support the the transparency of the FLOW methodology;
- Any use of the Sylou name or logo aimed to mislead users or in connection with content that violates law, harasses individuals, promotes hatred, or is otherwise harmful.  

## How to ask

Any doubt, email trademark@sylou.eu. This policy aims to be practical and pragmatic, and Sylou's goal is a healthy open-source ecosystem. If there are conditions worth discussing, or points worth clarifying, we can work them out together.

## Reporting concerns

If you encounter a use of the Sylou name, logo, or FLOW Pledge Badge format that seems off (such as a fork claiming to be official, a service misrepresenting its connection to the project, anything that might mislead users), feel free to flag it at trademark@sylou.eu. Most situations resolve through informal conversation.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on GitHub. Changes apply prospectively; permissions previously granted in writing remain in effect under their original terms.
