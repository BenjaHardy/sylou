Sylou Local

The whole of Sylou on your own computer, in Docker.
The guide: https://github.com/BenjaHardy/sylou/blob/main/docs/local.md

To get started, open a terminal in this folder and run:

  Windows (PowerShell)   .\sylou.cmd setup you@example.com
  Linux                  ./sylou setup you@example.com

It ends with a one-time link: open it, choose your password, then sign in
at http://localhost:8080. Run the same command on its own (.\sylou.cmd or
./sylou) to see everything else it can do.
